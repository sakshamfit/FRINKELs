# skills-janitor.md - Maintains and Improves Your AI OS Skills

## Purpose
Reviews, maintains, and improves your AI OS skill collection itself, ensuring your personal AI operating system remains effective, well-organized, and evolving with your needs.

## Triggers
- "review ai os skills"
- "skills janitor"
- "improve my skills"
- "maintain ai os"
- "update skill collection"

## Dependencies
Analyzes your existing AI OS skills in vault/AIOS/Skills/ to understand patterns, gaps, and opportunities for improvement.

## Instructions
1. **Inventory Skills**: List all skills in your AI OS collection with metadata
2. **Analyze Usage Patterns**: Identify which skills you use most/least frequently
3. **Identify Gaps**: Find missing skills that would complement your collection
4. **Assess Skill Quality**: Evaluate clarity, completeness, and effectiveness of each skill
5. **Check for Duplications**: Find overlapping or redundant skills
6. **Suggest Improvements**: Recommend updates, merges, splits, or new skills
7. **Maintain Organization**: Ensure skill naming, categorization, and formatting consistency
8. **Plan Evolution**: Suggest how your skill collection should grow with your changing needs

## Output
- Skills inventory with usage frequency and quality assessment
- Identified gaps in your skill collection
- Quality issues found in existing skills
- Duplication or overlap analysis
- Specific improvement suggestions for each skill
- Recommendations for new skills to add
- Organization and naming suggestions
- Evolution roadmap for your AI OS
